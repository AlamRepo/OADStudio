The Studio

Where architecture meets the everyday.

OAD Studio, also known as Omega Arc + Design, is an architecture and design practice based in Chandigarh, working across architecture, interior design, landscaping and modular solutions.

The studio describes its approach as integrated and turnkey, guiding clients through renovation and new-construction processes with attention to design, functionality and budget.
